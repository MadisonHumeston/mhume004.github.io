Electrical Engineer📚


I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of California, Riverside, with a strong interest in technology, innovation, and the development of solutions that can positively impact the future. My academic interests include electrical systems, nanotechnology, renewable energy, and emerging technologies. I am passionate about expanding my technical knowledge and applying what I learn in the classroom to real-world challenges. As I continue my education, I hope to build a career where I can contribute to meaningful technological advancements while encouraging greater representation of women in engineering and other STEM fields.

Throughout my academic experience, I have developed a foundation in electrical engineering principles, including circuit analysis, electromagnetics, and electrical components. My coursework and laboratory experiences have provided opportunities to work with electrical circuits, collect and interpret experimental data, troubleshoot problems, and apply theoretical concepts to practical situations. These experiences have strengthened my analytical and problem-solving abilities while teaching me the importance of accuracy, patience, and attention to detail. I have also learned the value of approaching difficult problems with persistence and curiosity. I believe that being a successful engineer requires more than technical knowledge; it also requires adaptability, creativity, and a willingness to continuously learn.

In addition to my academic background, I bring several years of professional experience working in a fast-paced customer service environment. I spent four years working at Kentucky Fried Chicken, where I developed strong communication, teamwork, time-management, and problem-solving skills. Working in a busy environment taught me how to remain organized under pressure, adapt to changing situations, and collaborate effectively with individuals who have different working styles and personalities. I learned the importance of being dependable, taking initiative, and maintaining a positive attitude while completing responsibilities efficiently. These experiences have shaped my work ethic and continue to influence how I approach both academic and professional opportunities.

I also value collaboration and supporting others in their learning experiences. Throughout my education, I have had opportunities to help classmates understand challenging course material and work through technical problems. I enjoy sharing knowledge, learning from others, and contributing to an environment where people feel comfortable asking questions and developing new skills. I believe communication and teamwork are essential qualities in engineering because successful projects often depend on individuals bringing different perspectives and strengths together.

Looking toward the future, I hope to pursue opportunities in technical development, engineering, and regulatory compliance, particularly in industries that focus on advancing technology and sustainable energy solutions. I am eager to gain hands-on experience, continue developing my technical abilities, and learn from professionals who share an interest in innovation. I am especially motivated by the possibility of contributing to renewable energy advancements and emerging technologies that can create a more sustainable future.

Ultimately, I strive to be a dedicated, dependable, and continuously growing professional. My experiences in both education and the workplace have taught me that success comes from consistency, determination, and being willing to step outside of my comfort zone. I look forward to using my education, skills, and experiences to make meaningful contributions to the engineering field while helping inspire and encourage future generations of women in STEM.
